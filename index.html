Our Story

15 Years of Better Sleep

Phoenix Beds was founded in 2009 with one simple mission: to give every British family access to a genuinely great night's sleep without the high-street markup.

We manufacture our mattresses right here in the UK, working with skilled craftspeople who take real pride in their work. Every spring, every layer of foam, every stitch of our covers is chosen for quality and longevity.
